## Who we're looking for

To keep standards high, we’re selecting local businesses that:  

  * Are locally owned and operated in the Sioux Falls area
  * Have a 4.5-star rating or higher on Google or another review platform
  * Are actively engaged in the community through offers, events, or partnerships
  * Only three businesses per category will be accepted.

## Frequently asked questions

Contact us to reserve your spot!

Why are you offering this level of exposure and a free Accessibility App?

Sioux Falls Go was built to connect trustworthy businesses with real locals. This offer sets the tone for how we plan to grow with businesses that lead with service, quality, and community give-back. We want to lead by example.

What if my business doesn’t have a 4.5-star review yet?

This standard is part of what helps us build trust with locals. If you’re close, feel free to reach out. We’ll look at your overall reputation and may suggest ways to qualify for future opportunities.

What are some good giveaway/gifts?

A local business can give a gift to their own service/shop or to another business. We've listed some giveaway ideas below:  

**Gift cards**  to local coffee shops, bakeries, and restaurants  
**Free treats**  like coffee, pastries, or desserts from your favorite local spots  
**Exclusive discounts**  on dining, shopping, and services  
**Special offers**  from any participating small businesses in Sioux Falls

Can national chains or franchises apply?

National chains and franchises may be eligible only if they are locally owned and operated. We prioritize Sioux Falls-rooted businesses because we want to build a thriving local economy supported by the community and the home-grown businesses that serve them.

What happens after the first year?

Once you’re a Founding Partner, you’re part of the family for life — that title never expires. If you choose not to renew, you’ll still keep your Founding Partner status (and the future discounts that come with it). The only thing you’ll lose is access to the premium features of your package, which will simply roll back to our standard service.
