### **“We Give Back” Banner**

How to earn the "We Give Back" banner:

  1. Participate in 1-2 giveaways a year 
  2. Offer discounts or specials for locals

### **Community Champion badge  **

How to earn the Community Champion badge:

  1. Participate in 4+ giveaways a year
  2. Offer discounts or specials for locals
  3. Upgrade to the [Community Champion package](https://siouxfallsgo.com/become-a-partner/)

Claim your banner & badge here

![Sioux Falls Go 2024 Local Guardian Banner](https://siouxfallsgo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/webguarbanner.png)

### **Local Guardian Badge**

How to earn the Local Guardian badge:

  1. This badge is available by invite-only
  2. Participate in 5+ giveaways per year
  3. Offer exclusive discount for the community 
  4. Have a 4.5+ star Google rating
  5. Support and collaborate with Local Guardians 
  6. Upgrade to the [Local Guardian package](https://siouxfallsgo.com/become-a-partner/)

Apply for consideration

## why participate?

Special discounts foster trust and increase traffic to local businesses, while giveaways are adventurous ways to encourage community involvement and highlight a company’s dedication to the area. 

Participation comes with a great deal of benefits, including exclusive promotions for our partners. Some opportunities are limited to one profession per industry. For more information on why you should participate, check out the [benefits page](https://siouxfallsgo.com/become-a-partner/) or contact us below.
