# Terms and Conditions

The following terms and conditions apply to all giveaways.

## No Guarantee of Value

The value of the giveaway is determined at the sole discretion of the business for which the giveaway was posted. There is no guaranteed value in a claimed giveaway regardless of what is printed, posted, or written on the giveaway, the website, or in email format.

If for any reason, the business is unable or unwilling to fulfill the giveaway, the giveaway has no cash value, cannot be returned, and cannot be refunded. The giveaway has no transferable value and can be rendered null and void at any time by the local business for which the giveaway was posted.

We personally meet with each business owner for which we post a giveaway and obtain their permission and approval to post the giveaway. But we also recognize that conditions can change quickly, especially for locally owned businesses. Therefore, **neither the business nor the business owners are under any obligation whatsoever to fulfill or comply with the giveaway**.

## Claim Frequency

No person may claim more than one giveaway every three months and no more than four giveaways within a 12-month period.

## No Cash Value

Giveaways have no cash value and cannot be exchanged or redeemed for cash at the local business.

## No Resale Value

Giveaways may not be sold or resold. You may freely transfer the giveaway to someone else, but you may not sell or post for sale any giveaway.

## 30-Day Giveaway Expiration

Unless otherwise explicitly stated in writing, a **claimed giveaway is valid for 30 days**  from the date it was awarded. After 30 days, the giveaway has no value and may no longer be redeemed at the local business.

## Contact

If you have any questions about the giveaways or about the Sioux Falls Local business directory, Contact Francis John using the contact form from the contact page.
