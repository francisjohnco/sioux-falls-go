### about Sioux Falls Go

Sioux Falls Go is an exciting new platform where customers can uncover special offers while supporting businesses. Local businesses can connect with more local customers in a new and fun way.

We make exploring Sioux Falls a fun and rewarding adventure. Not only can you discover new (or new-to-you) local businesses, but you can also enter to win cool prizes from our partner companies! 

Businesses that participate in our Spotlight Giveaways and support our goal of bringing the community together are rewarded through increased visibility and exciting growth opportunities. By working together, not only are we helping continue to make Sioux Falls a thriving community, but one we can share.
