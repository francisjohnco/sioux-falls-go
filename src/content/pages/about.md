The best businesses in Sioux Falls are not always the ones with the biggest ad budgets. They are the plumber who calls you back the same day, the coffee shop that remembers your order, the family business that has been fixing things right for twenty years without ever buying a billboard. Sioux Falls Go is where you find them.

## Free to be listed, but invitation only

Being listed on Sioux Falls Go costs a business nothing, but not every business gets in. Every business here has to meet the same standard before we list it.

- **Locally owned** and serving the Sioux Falls area. National chains and online only businesses are not eligible.
- **Highly rated,** with an active Google Business Profile at 4.8 stars or higher. That is a real requirement, not a suggestion.
- **Willing to give back** by taking part in community giveaways.
- **Ready for new customers,** open and able to take on more work right now.

A business that does not meet the standard is not listed, no matter what it offers to pay. That means you are not looking at whoever spent the most to be here. You are looking at local owners who earned their spot by doing good work and are willing to stand behind it.

## A real conversation, not a form

When a business is a fit, we set up a call and actually talk with the owner. What do they do, what makes them different, what are they proud of? That conversation becomes their profile. It is why our profiles read like a neighbor telling you about a business they trust instead of a sales pitch.

## Knowing the people behind a business matters

When you know who owns the business you are hiring, you get a higher level of accountability. A local owner's name is on the work, their reputation travels by word of mouth, and they will see you at the grocery store. Every profile tells you who is behind the business and why they do what they do.

## Verified and Not Verified

Every profile shows a label at the top. **Verified** means our team has personally confirmed the business and the details on its profile. **Not Verified** means we listed it from public information and have not confirmed it with the owner yet. [Read exactly what each label means.](/verified)

## We like having fun

Sioux Falls Go runs [Spotlight Giveaways](/spotlight-giveaway) where you can win prizes from local businesses, collects real offers on our [Local Deals](/local-deals) page, and sends a Spotlight postcard to Sioux Falls homes with deals you can only get through the mailer. It is all a way to help you discover a local business you did not know about yet.

## How Sioux Falls Go stays free

Sioux Falls Go is run by SAJMedia, a local marketing agency here in Sioux Falls. Some businesses that already meet our standard choose to invest in Sioux Falls Go as [Community Champions](/community-champion). They get extra features like spotlight articles and sponsored guides, they fund bigger giveaways, and they are always marked with the Community Champion seal so you know. That support is what keeps the guides, giveaways, and listings free for everyone else.

## Own a great local business?

If your business is locally owned, rated 4.8 stars or higher, and ready to give back, [apply to be featured](/get-featured). It takes about two minutes and costs nothing.
