## Robust Local ECONOMY 

We’re on a mission to unite local businesses and the community to create a robust local economy. It starts with supporting each other. Our distinctive badging system makes it easy. It helps identify local businesses that give back so we can help keep their doors open.

## The " Gives Back" Banner

To be awarded the "Gives Back" banner, a local business or organization must engage in community building activities or provide incentives for locals. The "Gives Back" banner, in contrast to the community champion and local guardian badges, is utilized exclusively on our website.

Unlike other recognition that is given without merit, businesses that earn this exclusive banner have demonstrated real-world efforts in engaging the community by participating in variety of engagement initiatives, such as offering specials to the community and participating in giveaways. 

This banner is an effort to distinguish these partners from the rest of the directory listings for more visibility and recognization and make it easier for local consumers to find them. When you support these local businesses, you are also supporting the community.

![](https://siouxfallsgo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/brown-wooden-arrow-signed.jpeg)

## Community Champion

Businesses and organizations that participate in **three** giveaways annually and offer special deals to the community are awarded the title: Sioux Falls Community Champion. A business given this title also gets the community champion badge showcasing its commitment to the community. 

They can place this badge on their business page, their website, and on their shop windows to differentiate themselves from other businesses. In addition, we’ll promote these businesses on siouxfallsgo.com for more visibility, growth opportunities, and local recognition. 

Unlike other titles which are purely for show, businesses that earn the Sioux Falls Community Champion title have both demonstrated their commitment to community growth and have successfully implemented their efforts for real-world impact. 

Not only do they care about the community, but they have also proven their commitment by giving back and fostering engagement for greater connection and future growth. Support these local heroes to help grow the community in an innovative way. Together, let's make Sioux Falls even better. 

![Sioux Falls Go 2024 community guardian banner](https://siouxfallsgo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/webcommbanner.png)

## Local Guardian 

The guardian badge is given to the ultimate supporters of our community. These local legends are committed to supporting our vision to engage the community and create a robust local economy. They do it by:

  * Encouraging local consumers to shop locally 
  * Participating in the Spotlight Giveaways 
  * Providing exclusive discounts to locals.

These collaborations demonstrate a dedication to the community while encouraging its growth in a dynamic way. Local Guardians help inspire local businesses to support our city and improve Sioux Falls by adopting our vision to connect Sioux Falls for sustainable growth. 

Unlike other titles that are purely for show, Sioux Falls Local Guardians have proven their dedication by supporting Sioux Falls Go and the exposure it provides to outstanding local companies and organizations in the neighborhood.

Ready to jump in on the action? Check out the perks given to our Local Guardians and join us in continuing Sioux Falls success!
