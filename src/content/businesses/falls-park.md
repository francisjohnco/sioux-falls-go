Falls Park is Sioux Falls' namesake and most visited attraction, a 128-acre park where the Big Sioux River cascades over pink quartzite at 7,400 gallons per second. Rated 4.7 on Tripadvisor from over 3,400 reviews, visitors ride the elevator up the observation tower and climb the rocks along the shoreline. Free and open year-round.
