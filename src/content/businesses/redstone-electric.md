# Reliable Electrical Work in Sioux Falls That Prioritizes Safety and Clear Communication

When you hire an electrician, you are trusting someone with more than wires and breakers. You are trusting them with the safety of your home, your business, and the people inside it. Working with Redstone Electric feels grounded in that responsibility from the very beginning.

Owned by Jacob Munce, Redstone Electric serves Sioux Falls with a practical and straightforward approach that focuses on quality workmanship, honest communication, and installations that are done the right way. Whether you need help with residential electrical upgrades or commercial service work, the experience is designed to feel dependable and easy to understand.

## What to Expect

One of the first things people tend to notice is the communication. Projects move smoother when expectations are clear, timelines are explained, and questions are answered without confusion. That steady communication helps remove a lot of the stress people often associate with electrical work.

Services range from residential installations like lighting, EV chargers, electric heaters, and hot tubs to a variety of commercial electrical services. The focus is not simply getting something to work. It is making sure the installation is safe, properly planned, and completed according to electrical code.

Jacob believes there is a major difference between making something function and making sure it is truly safe for the long term. That mindset shapes the entire experience. Attention is placed on doing the work carefully, following code requirements, and helping property owners feel confident in the finished result.

## Meet Jacob Munce

Jacob was born and raised in Sioux Falls and has spent more than a decade in the electrical trade. From the beginning, his goal was to eventually build a company of his own where he could provide excellent service and quality work without cutting corners.

Today, that vision continues to shape Redstone Electric. As a licensed electrical contractor, Jacob brings both technical knowledge and a strong sense of responsibility to every project.
