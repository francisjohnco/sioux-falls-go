McKennan Park anchors one of Sioux Falls' oldest and most charming neighborhoods, with century-old trees, manicured gardens, a historic bandshell, and a summer swimming pool. Visitors describe it as a peaceful, well-maintained escape for a walk, a picnic, or watching the resident squirrels, with free admission year-round.
