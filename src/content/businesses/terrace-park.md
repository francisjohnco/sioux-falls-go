Terrace Park is home to the historic Shoto-Teien Japanese Gardens, originally designed in the 1920s, alongside a family aquatic center, a lake for fishing, and sports fields. Visitors describe the gardens as a genuinely serene, photo-worthy escape, with the aquatic center a favorite for families in summer.
