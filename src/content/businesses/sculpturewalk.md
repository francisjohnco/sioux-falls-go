SculptureWalk fills downtown Sioux Falls with rotating outdoor sculptures, free to view year-round along Phillips Avenue, with each piece available for purchase. A 2023 Tripadvisor Travelers' Choice Award winner placing it in the top 10% of attractions worldwide, visitors describe it as a relaxing, self-guided walk through the heart of downtown.
