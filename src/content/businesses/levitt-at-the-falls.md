Levitt at the Falls hosts 50 free outdoor concerts each summer on a grass berm overlooking Falls Park, seating up to 5,000 and drawing both local favorites and internationally known acts. Reviewers consistently describe it as a beloved, family-friendly community experience, with clean facilities, food trucks, and lawn chair rentals available.
