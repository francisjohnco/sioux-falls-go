The Great Plains Zoo spans 45 acres with over 1,000 animals across 137 species, from Asian cats to Galapagos tortoises, alongside the Delbridge Museum of Natural History. Families describe it as a manageable size for a few hours with young kids, though some feel admission runs high for what's on display.
