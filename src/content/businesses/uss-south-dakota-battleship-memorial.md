The USS South Dakota Battleship Memorial marks the outline of the most decorated battleship of World War II with a free museum of artifacts, photos, and ship pieces. Visitors describe it as a moving, well-run stop for history buffs and veterans, worth about an hour. The grounds stay open year-round; the museum runs May through September.
