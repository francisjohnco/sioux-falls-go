The Big Sioux Recreation Trail is a paved, 19-mile loop circling Sioux Falls along the Big Sioux River, with over 17 miles of connecting spurs to neighborhoods. Rated 4.6 on AllTrails from nearly 900 reviews, users describe it as flat, well-maintained, and family-friendly, with the highlight passing through Falls Park.
