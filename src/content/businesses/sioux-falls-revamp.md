Sioux Falls Revamp is a home renovation resource built specifically for Sioux Falls homeowners, not a contractor pitching its own services. The site runs a growing library of 30-plus local renovation guides covering kitchens, bathrooms, basements, historic homes, and whole-home projects, all written for South Dakota's climate and housing stock rather than generic national advice.

Its centerpiece is real return-on-investment data pulled from actual Minnehaha and Lincoln County closings, available as a free annual ROI report and an interactive calculator that estimates payback by project type, budget, and neighborhood. The site also runs a vetted local pro directory covering general contractors, kitchen and bath specialists, basement finishers, historic restoration experts, and architects, with every listing checked for genuine Sioux Falls presence rather than a national chain dressed up as local. A weekly newsletter rounds it out with market trends, new guides, and contractor spotlights.

Sioux Falls Revamp is a genuinely useful starting point for homeowners planning a renovation, before they ever pick up the phone to call a contractor.
