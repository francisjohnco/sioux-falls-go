The Pettigrew Home & Museum offers free guided tours of the restored 1889 Queen Anne home of Richard Pettigrew, South Dakota's first full-term U.S. senator, alongside artifacts he collected from around the world. Visitors describe it as a genuine hidden gem worth an hour in the historic Cathedral District.
