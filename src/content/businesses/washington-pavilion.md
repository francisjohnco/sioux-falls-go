The Washington Pavilion combines a science discovery center, art museum, IMAX-style CineDome, and Broadway theater under one roof in a beautifully renovated historic high school building. Families describe spending 3 to 4 hours easily, especially at the hands-on Kirby Science Discovery Center, and the theater regularly hosts touring Broadway shows.
