question: "When should I actually worry about a tripping breaker?"
category: "electricians"
hub: ["local-service-faqs"]
featured: true
relatedBusinesses: ["redstone-electric"]
---

If the same breaker trips repeatedly, or trips without an obvious change in what's plugged in, that's different from routine overload — it can indicate a wiring or connection issue, and it's worth a same-week inspection rather than continuing to reset it.
