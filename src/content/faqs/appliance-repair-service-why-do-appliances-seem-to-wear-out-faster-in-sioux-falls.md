---
question: "Why do appliances seem to wear out faster in Sioux Falls?"
category: "appliance-repair-service"
hub: ["local-service-faqs"]
featured: true
relatedBusinesses: ["the-appliance-doc"]
---

Hard water, commonly in the 12.8-15 grains per gallon range here, accelerates scale buildup in water heaters, dishwashers, and washing machines, which is a real reason these appliances often need service sooner than in areas with softer water.
