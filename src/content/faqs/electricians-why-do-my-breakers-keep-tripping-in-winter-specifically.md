question: "Why do my breakers keep tripping in winter specifically?"
category: "electricians"
hub: ["local-service-faqs"]
featured: true
relatedBusinesses: ["redstone-electric"]
---

Cold-weather load is the usual cause — block heaters, space heaters, and heat tape all draw hard current at the same time overnight when temperatures drop below zero, which is routine in a Sioux Falls January. A single trip during a genuine cold snap is often the breaker working correctly, not a sign of failure.
