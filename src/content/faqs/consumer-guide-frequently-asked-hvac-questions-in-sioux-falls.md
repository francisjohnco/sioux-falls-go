**How often should I service my HVAC system?**

Most HVAC systems should be serviced twice per year:

  * Once in the spring for air conditioning
  * Once in the fall for heating

Regular maintenance improves efficiency, prevents breakdowns, and extends system lifespan.

### When should I replace my furnace or air conditioner?

Most systems last:

  * Furnaces: 15–20 years
  * Air conditioners: 10–15 years

You may need replacement if your system:

  * Requires frequent repairs
  * Has rising energy bills
  * Struggles to heat or cool evenly
  * Uses outdated refrigerant

A local HVAC professional can help you evaluate your options.

### Why is my home unevenly heated or cooled?

Common causes include:

  * Poor insulation
  * Leaky ductwork
  * Dirty air filters
  * Improper system sizing
  * Blocked vents

An inspection can identify the issue and restore comfort.

### How much does HVAC repair cost in Sioux Falls?

Costs vary based on the problem, but typical ranges include:

  * Minor repairs: $100–$300
  * Moderate repairs: $300–$700
  * Major repairs: $700+

Always request a written estimate before approving work.

### Should I repair or replace my HVAC system?

A good rule is the 50% rule:

If repair costs exceed 50% of a new system’s value, replacement may be smarter long-term.

Other factors include age, efficiency, and comfort level.

### How can I lower my heating and cooling bills?

You can reduce energy costs by:

  * Changing filters regularly
  * Sealing air leaks
  * Installing a programmable thermostat
  * Scheduling routine maintenance
  * Upgrading to high-efficiency equipment

Small changes can produce big savings over time.

### What size HVAC system does my home need?

System size depends on:

  * Square footage
  * Insulation quality
  * Ceiling height
  * Window placement
  * Local climate

Proper sizing requires a professional load calculation. Oversized or undersized systems waste energy and reduce comfort.

### Is it better to hire a local HVAC company?

Yes. Local companies understand:

  * Sioux Falls climate conditions
  * Regional building codes
  * Seasonal demand patterns
  * Local utility programs

They’re also easier to reach for ongoing support.

### What should I look for in an HVAC contractor?

Choose a company that offers:

  * Proper licensing and insurance
  * Local references
  * Transparent pricing
  * Written warranties
  * Emergency service options

Avoid contractors who pressure you into quick decisions.

### Do HVAC systems affect indoor air quality?

Yes. HVAC systems help control:

  * Dust and allergens
  * Humidity levels
  * Air circulation
  * Mold growth

Upgrades like air purifiers, humidifiers, and high-quality filters can improve indoor air quality.
