**Sioux Falls Go. Where Your Dollars Build Community**

In Sioux Falls, shopping local has always mattered. Now, it matters more than ever.

Because when you choose a business through Sioux Falls Go, you are not only supporting local. You are supporting businesses that care about the community and consistently show it through generosity, service, and excellence.

Buying local already keeps money in our city. Sioux Falls Go makes it even better by helping you instantly see which businesses go the extra mile and rewarding you for choosing them.

This is why Sioux Falls Go exists. To make supporting local meaningful, simple, and fun.

⸻

### **The Power of Gives Back Businesses**

When you see the **Gives Back** banner on a Sioux Falls Go listing, you are looking at a business that has already done something positive for the community.

This badge is earned by participating in a Sioux Falls Go community giveaway. It can be a service, a product, a gift card, or any meaningful contribution that brings joy to local families.

These are the businesses that show up for Sioux Falls in real and practical ways.

They donate, give, share, and support. Their generosity creates a ripple effect that makes our city stronger. When you support a Gives Back business, you are choosing people who put Sioux Falls first.

⸻

### **Community Champions**

The **Community Champion** badge highlights businesses that go beyond a single giveaway.

These partners not only give back, they also offer something special to the Sioux Falls community such as a perk, an incentive, or exclusive local value.

Community Champions demonstrate kindness, consistency, and a commitment to serving people well.

They earn increased visibility inside Sioux Falls Go and are regularly featured in our spotlight campaigns.

They are trusted by the community and recognized for their willingness to invest in Sioux Falls in meaningful ways.

⸻

### **City Authority. The Best of Sioux Falls**

**City Authority** is the highest recognition inside Sioux Falls Go.

It is not purchased. It is earned through excellence.

These businesses have proven leadership, strong community trust, and a consistent record of serving Sioux Falls with integrity.

They meet all requirements for Gives Back and Community Champion and then rise even further.

City Authority partners also receive opportunities to publish expert articles that help educate the community and strengthen their reputation as leaders in their field.

This badge is displayed only on the website and makes it simple for locals to find the most reliable professionals in each category.

⸻

## **It Is Not Just Shopping. It Is a Local Adventure.**

Sioux Falls Go is more than a directory. It is a community-driven experience.

With giveaways, local spotlights, nominations, and curated listings built on values, it becomes easy and enjoyable to support the people who support our city.

No more guessing.

No more outdated lists.

Just a clear and confident way to find trustworthy local businesses.

Every time you choose a partner through Sioux Falls Go, you are supporting dreams, uplifting neighbors, and building a stronger Sioux Falls.

⸻

## **How to Use Sioux Falls Go Like a Local Legend**

**1\. Look for the Badges**  
Gives Back, Community Champion, and City Authority highlight businesses that deserve your support.

**2\. Join the Giveaways**  
Locals win prizes simply for participating. It brings fun and rewards into supporting local.

**3\. Use the Directory Before You Buy**  
Find trusted businesses that invest in the community and take pride in serving Sioux Falls.

**4\. Support Businesses That Support You**  
Reviews matter, but values matter more. Choose businesses that demonstrate real impact.

**5\. Share the Love**  
Tag the businesses you support, leave reviews, and help lift up the people who make Sioux Falls special.
