We're building something unique. Get in on [the action](https://siouxfallsgo.com/start-here/) at the ground level. Here is our roadmap**:**

## Phase 1 – Build the Foundation

  * Soft launch Sioux Falls Go for service providers
  * [Curate Top 10 lists in major local industries](https://siouxfallsgo.com/sioux-falls-top-picks/)
  * Begin qualifying businesses for greater exposure
  * Populate the directory with top-rated businesses (4.7+ stars)
  * Engage community nomination for featured spots
  * Businesses can claim badges ("**Gives Back** "), upgrade, and optimize listing pages

## Phase 2 – Expand & Engage the Community

  * Create and publish a Public Roadmap with update logs
  * Open Sioux Falls Go direct mail service: local offers + guides
  * Launch the Local Legends newsletter with exclusive insight, limited time offers, and more
  * Start weekly giveaways to drive engagement
  * Launch exclusive ZIP code marketing model
  * Publish SEO-optimized content for each industry 

## Phase 3 – Deepen Local Loyalty & Utility

  * Campaign for user review of businesses pages
  * Let users build their “My Local Favorites” list
  * Local Business Spotlight (featured content)
  * Community Champion upgrades with perks

## Phase 4 – Future-Oriented Expansion

  * Add job board/gigs from featured businesses
  *  Integrate a local deals feed (email exclusive)
  * Launch a mobile or progressive web app
  * City ambassador program for outreach
