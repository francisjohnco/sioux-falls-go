Interior painting in Sioux Falls typically runs $2–$4 per square foot including labor and materials, while exterior painting runs higher — often $3,000–$8,000 for a full single-family home, depending on siding material and how much prep work old paint requires.

## Typical price ranges

- **Single room, interior:** $300–$800 depending on size and number of coats
- **Whole-home interior:** $3,000–$7,000+ for an average Sioux Falls home
- **Exterior, full home:** $3,000–$8,000, more for homes with extensive trim work or siding in poor condition
- **Cabinet refinishing:** $2,000–$5,000 depending on kitchen size

## Why timing matters here more than in milder climates

Exterior paint needs a real window of consistently warm, dry weather to cure properly — and Sioux Falls's usable exterior painting season is genuinely shorter than in southern states. Most reputable painters won't schedule exterior work much before May or much after September, which means demand (and often price) clusters in that window. Booking an exterior job in early spring, before the summer rush, can sometimes mean better scheduling and pricing.

## What actually moves the price

- **Prep work.** Peeling, cracked, or previously lead-painted surfaces (common on homes built before 1978) require more prep time, which is often the real cost driver, not the paint itself.
- **Siding material.** Wood siding generally costs more to prep and paint than vinyl or fiber cement.
- **Number of colors and trim detail.** More color changes and detailed trim work add labor hours.

## Getting a fair quote

Ask specifically what prep work is included — scraping, sanding, priming bare spots — since a lower bid that skips proper prep often means a paint job that fails within a year or two, especially through a Sioux Falls winter freeze-thaw cycle.
