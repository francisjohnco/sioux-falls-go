Recurring house cleaning in Sioux Falls typically runs $100-$200 per visit for an average home, with bi-weekly or monthly service usually priced lower per visit than one-time bookings.

## Typical price ranges

- **Recurring cleaning (bi-weekly/monthly):** $100-$200 per visit for an average home
- **One-time deep clean:** $200-$400, more for homes not regularly maintained
- **Move-in/move-out cleaning:** $250-$450, given the thoroughness required
- **Post-construction cleanup:** varies widely, often $300+

## What actually moves the price

- **Number of bathrooms**, which take disproportionately more cleaning time than the square footage would suggest.
- **Frequency.** Recurring service is priced lower per visit than one-time bookings since the home stays in better maintained condition between visits.
- **Whether it's a first visit.** A first deep clean often costs more than subsequent recurring visits, since it typically addresses buildup a regular schedule would have prevented.

## Getting a fair quote

Ask whether the quote assumes a first-visit deep clean or ongoing maintenance pricing — some companies quote low to win the first booking, then price recurring visits differently. Getting this distinction clear upfront avoids a surprise on your second invoice.
