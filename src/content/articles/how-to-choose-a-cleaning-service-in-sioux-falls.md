Cleaning services work in your home, often unsupervised, which makes trust and verification matter more here than for services performed while you're present.

## What to verify first

- **Insurance and bonding**, specifically. Bonding covers theft in a way standard insurance doesn't — worth asking about directly given cleaners typically work without direct supervision.
- **Background checks on staff**, if hiring a company rather than an individual.
- **Real local reviews**, ideally from customers who've used them for recurring service, not just a single visit.

## Questions worth asking

1. **"Are your cleaners bonded, not just insured?"** This distinction matters specifically because of the unsupervised access involved.
2. **"Will the same person or team clean my home each time?"** Consistency matters for both quality and comfort with having someone in your home regularly.
3. **"What's your policy if something gets damaged during cleaning?"** A clear answer here, rather than a vague one, is a good sign.
4. **"Do you bring your own supplies, or do you use mine?"** Relevant for cost, allergies, and specific product preferences.

## Red flags

- No bonding, or unclear answers about what bonding covers
- No consistent staff — a different unfamiliar person every visit with no explanation
- Reluctance to provide local references
- Pricing dramatically below every other quote with no explanation

## Building trust over time

For a first booking with a new company, being present for the initial visit is a reasonable way to build comfort before deciding whether to leave them unsupervised on future visits — most reputable Sioux Falls cleaning companies expect this and won't be offended by it.
