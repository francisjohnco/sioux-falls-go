## Why is Sioux Falls soil harder on lawns than other places?

Much of the area sits on expansive clay soil, which compacts easily and drains less efficiently than sandy or loamy soil — that's why aeration and soil-aware fertilization programs matter more here than generic national lawn advice accounts for.

## How much does weekly mowing cost?

Typically $35-$60 per visit for an average residential lot, sometimes with a modest discount for a season-long agreement versus pay-per-visit.

## Why does my lawn look patchy every spring?

Winter freeze-thaw cycles stress grass roots more in clay soil, making spring patchiness a common and largely normal pattern rather than a sign of a problem.

## When should I aerate my lawn?

Fall is generally considered the stronger window for cool-season grasses common in Sioux Falls, though spring aeration is a reasonable option if fall was missed.

## Do I need a licensed company for weed and pest treatments?

The company should be properly licensed for chemical application under South Dakota regulations — worth confirming directly if their services include pesticide or herbicide treatments.

## Is a full-season contract worth it over paying per visit?

Often it comes with a modest per-visit discount, but confirm cancellation terms before committing, especially if your schedule or budget might change mid-season.
