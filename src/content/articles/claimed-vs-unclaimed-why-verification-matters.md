Every business on Sioux Falls Go shows one of two badges: **Verified** or **Not Verified**. It sits right on the category page, right next to your name, in the exact same spot every visitor looks first.

That one word does more work than almost anything else on your profile.

## The two listings side by side

Here's the honest difference between a claimed, verified listing and one that's still sitting unclaimed.

**Unclaimed / Not Verified**
- Shows a gray "Not Verified" badge on every card and profile page
- Basic info only — whatever was available when the listing was first created
- No say in how your business is described
- Excluded from the homepage spotlight, which only ever shows recently verified listings
- Just another name in the directory

**Claimed / Verified**
- Shows the green "Verified" badge — the same trust signal visitors already recognize from every other listing on the site
- A profile written from an actual conversation with you, not a generic template
- Eligible for the homepage spotlight and priority placement across the site
- Access to the RankAPro app, so real customer reviews keep building your reputation automatically instead of sitting still
- A business we can genuinely stand behind, because we've actually talked to you

## Why "Verified" matters more than it looks

**Authenticity.** An unclaimed listing is our best guess at who you are. A verified one is confirmed, straight from you. Visitors can tell the difference, even if they couldn't explain how.

**Trust.** The whole reason Sioux Falls Go exists is that a directory full of unverified listings isn't actually useful — anyone can claim to be the best plumber in town. A verified badge tells a visitor someone actually checked.

**Branding.** Your unclaimed listing describes your business the way we found it. Your verified listing describes it the way you'd describe it to a neighbor — in your own words, with what actually makes you different.

**Local SEO.** Verified businesses get pulled into related articles, FAQ pages, and neighborhood guides across the site, which means more real links pointing back at your profile — not just one static listing sitting alone.

**Customization.** An unclaimed profile is fixed. A verified one is yours to shape — the photo, the description, the details that actually matter to a customer deciding between you and the business next to you.

**Priority promotion.** We simply promote verified listings harder, on the homepage and throughout the site. It's not favoritism — it's that we can only vouch for what we've actually confirmed. An unverified listing is a guess. A verified one is something we can stand behind.

## What claiming actually gets you

Once you claim your listing, you're also connected to the RankAPro app — the same review management system that helps a business turn its actual good service into actual good reviews, automatically, instead of hoping happy customers remember to leave one on their own.

This isn't a paid upgrade. It's a short conversation to confirm you're really you, and to write your profile the way it should have been written from the start.
