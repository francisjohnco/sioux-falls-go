## How much does window cleaning cost for an average Sioux Falls home?

Exterior-only typically runs $150-$250, with interior and exterior combined running $250-$400, depending on window count and accessibility.

## Why do Sioux Falls windows get so dirty in winter?

Road salt spray and de-icing sand accumulate on exterior glass over the cold months, often more heavily than typical seasonal dust buildup in milder climates.

## Can windows be cleaned when it's freezing outside?

It's possible but not ideal — cleaning solution can freeze on the glass before it's wiped away, causing streaking. Most companies prefer temperatures consistently above freezing.

## Are screens and window tracks included in a standard cleaning?

Often not automatically — these are commonly quoted as add-ons, so confirm what's included before assuming a lower quote covers the same scope as a higher one.

## Should window cleaners be insured for second-story work?

Yes — if your home has second-story windows, confirm the company's insurance specifically covers ladder or lift work, not just general liability.

## Is it worth cleaning windows twice a year?

Many Sioux Falls homeowners do — once in spring to address winter buildup, and again in fall to maximize natural light through the shorter winter days ahead.
