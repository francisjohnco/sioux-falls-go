A garage door that has jumped its tracks feels urgent, especially when your car is stuck inside or the door is hanging at an odd angle. The good news is that the right response in the first few minutes can keep a manageable problem from becoming a dangerous one. The wrong response, mainly trying to force the door open or run the opener, can turn a repair into a replacement. For a full picture of garage door problems and repair in Sioux Falls, see [Garage Door Repair in Sioux Falls, the Complete Guide](/guides/garage-door-repair-sioux-falls).

## Why a Garage Door Comes Off Track

There is almost always a clear cause, and knowing it matters because the cause affects what the repair will involve.

**Common reasons a garage door came off rollers:**

- A car bumped the door while it was closed, bending the bottom section or the track itself
- A cable snapped or went slack, letting one side of the door drop unevenly (read more at [Broken Garage Door Cable, Causes and Repair](/guides/broken-garage-door-cable))
- A roller wore down, cracked, or broke and simply fell out of the track
- Something blocked the door's path mid-travel, a chunk of ice, a tool, a recycling bin
- The track itself bent, shifted, or came loose from the wall bracket

In Sioux Falls, cold weather adds its own pressure. A National Weather Service meteorologist described the February 2021 cold snap as the longest of its kind since about 1996, according to SiouxFalls.Business. Metal contracts in extreme cold, lubricant thickens, and springs and cables are already under higher stress. That combination raises the odds of a roller failing or a cable snapping right when you're leaving for work on a minus-fifteen morning.

Freeze-thaw cycles also heave garage floors and threshold areas over time. If the floor has shifted even slightly, a door that tracked fine for years can suddenly catch and jump.

## Stop. Do Not Run the Opener.

This is the most important instruction on this page.

If you can see that the door is off track, or if the door made a loud bang and then moved unevenly, disconnect the opener and do not press the button again. Running an opener against a door that is jammed in the track puts enormous stress on the motor, the drive mechanism, and whatever is still holding the door up. It can cause the door to come down fast and hard.

Garage doors are heavy. A standard two-car steel door can weigh 150 to 200 pounds or more. When the door is off track, that weight is no longer distributed evenly across the spring and cable system. One section may be held by a single roller or by nothing at all.

Do not try to muscle the door open by hand either. If a cable is broken or the spring is under tension on one side only, pulling on the door can cause a sudden drop or a cable to snap loose and whip.

The right move is to leave the door exactly where it is until a technician arrives.

## How to Secure the Door Right Now

You cannot always leave a half-open or visibly damaged door unattended, especially overnight or in a Sioux Falls winter when wind and blowing snow become a factor.

**Steps to stabilize the situation safely:**

- If the door is fully closed and stuck, leave it closed. That is the safest position.
- If the door is partially open and you need to secure your garage, use locking pliers (vise-grips) clamped onto the track just below the bottom roller on each side. This prevents the door from sliding further down.
- Do not use a bungee cord, rope, or anything with stretch. If tension releases suddenly, the door can drop.
- If your car is inside and you need to get it out, check [Emergency Garage Door Repair in Sioux Falls, What to Expect](/guides/emergency-garage-door-repair-sioux-falls) for guidance on safely getting a vehicle out without making the door situation worse.
- Unplug the opener from the ceiling outlet so no one accidentally triggers it.

If the door is hanging at an angle and you can see exposed cables or a bent section, do not reach in to adjust anything. That is a job for a technician with the right tools and the knowledge to relieve spring tension safely.

## What the Repair Actually Involves

An off-track repair is not just sliding the door back into the track. A technician will work through the following:

**What a typical visit covers:**

- Identifying the root cause (broken roller, bent track, snapped cable, bent section)
- Releasing or supporting spring tension before any repositioning happens
- Straightening or replacing the track if it bent
- Replacing worn or broken rollers, not just reseating the ones that fell out
- Checking cables and drums for fraying or uneven winding
- Testing the door's balance before reconnecting the opener

If a cable caused the door to come off track, that cable needs replacement before the door goes back on. If the bottom panel took a hard hit from a car, the technician will assess whether the section can be straightened or needs to be replaced. Bent tracks sometimes need replacement too, especially if the bend is sharp.

Nationally, off-track repairs range widely depending on what caused the problem. A simple roller-and-retrack job is on the lower end; a bent track plus a broken cable plus a damaged panel is a significantly larger job. Get a written quote before any work begins. ## Choosing Who to Call in Sioux Falls

Not every company advertising garage door repair is locally based or properly licensed.

The City of Sioux Falls licenses residential contractors, and since 2024 has also offered a Residential Roofing and Repair Contractor license for smaller jobs including door replacements on homes and townhomes requiring no structural changes, according to the City of Sioux Falls in February 2024 and April 2026. The city has not specifically listed garage door track repair as requiring a permit for a same-size, same-opening repair, but if a technician needs to widen the opening or change the header, that is structural work and a permit is required. When in doubt, call Planning and Development Services at 605-367-8670.

You can verify whether a contractor holds a city license at siouxfalls.gov/licensed-contractors or by calling Building Services at 605-367-8672. When you call a company, ask which city license they hold. A legitimate local company will answer that question without hesitation.

**Other questions to ask before you book:**

- Do you charge a separate diagnostic or service call fee?
- Is the quote all-in, or are parts billed separately?
- Do you offer after-hours or weekend service, and what is the added fee?
- What warranty do you provide on parts and labor?

Several Sioux Falls garage door companies do offer evening and weekend service. Some charge a premium for it. Ask before they arrive so there are no surprises on the invoice.
