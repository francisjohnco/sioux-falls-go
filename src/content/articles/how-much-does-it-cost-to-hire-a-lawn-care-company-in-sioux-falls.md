Weekly mowing for an average Sioux Falls residential lot typically runs $35-$60 per visit, while a full-season fertilization and weed control program runs $300-$600 for the year, depending on lawn size and how many applications are included.

## Typical price ranges

- **Weekly mowing:** $35-$60 per visit for an average lot
- **Full-season fertilization program (4-6 applications):** $300-$600/year
- **Aeration:** $75-$150 per visit, typically once or twice a year
- **Spring/fall cleanup:** $150-$400 depending on yard size and debris volume

## Why Sioux Falls's clay soil affects the cost

Much of the Sioux Falls area sits on expansive clay soil, which compacts more easily than sandy or loamy soil and drains less efficiently. That's part of why aeration is recommended more consistently here than in regions with naturally looser soil — compacted clay makes it harder for grass roots to access water and nutrients, which can mean more fertilization needed to get the same results.

## What actually moves the price

- **Lot size and lawn complexity.** More flower beds, slopes, and obstacles increase mowing time.
- **Whether you want organic or synthetic treatment programs** — organic options often cost more per application.
- **Contract length.** Season-long agreements sometimes come with modest per-visit savings versus one-off bookings.

## Getting a fair quote

Ask whether the fertilization program is based on a soil test or a generic schedule. A company that tests your specific soil before recommending treatments is working from real data about your lawn, not a one-size-fits-all program — which matters more here given how variable clay soil conditions can be even within the same neighborhood.
