These are the questions Sioux Falls business owners ask us most about marketing, answered plainly. Each answer links to a deeper guide when you want more.

## Getting Found

### How does Google decide which local businesses to show?

Google says local results are based mainly on three things: relevance, distance, and prominence.

Relevance is how well your profile matches the search. Distance is how close you are to the person searching. Prominence is how well known you are, which includes links from other websites and your reviews. More in [How to Show Up in the Google Map Pack in Sioux Falls](/guides/google-map-pack-sioux-falls).

### Can I pay Google to rank higher in the map results?

No. Google says there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Ads can appear above the results, but they are labeled as ads and stop when you stop paying.

### What is the most important thing to fix first?

For most local businesses, it is the Google Business Profile. A complete, accurate profile with real photos and steady reviews helps you get found and get chosen at the same time.

Our [Google Business Profile Checklist](/guides/google-business-profile-checklist-sioux-falls) walks through every setting.

### Do I still need a website?

Yes, but its job has changed. It proves you are real, tells Google and AI tools what you do, and turns people who already found you into calls.

It rarely brings in customers all by itself anymore. See [What Your Website Really Does for a Sioux Falls Business](/guides/what-your-website-does-for-a-local-business).

### Will ChatGPT or Google's AI recommend my business?

They can. AI tools lean on what the web already says about you: your website, your reviews, your listings, and mentions on trusted local sites. Clear information and real reviews make you easier to recommend.

## Reviews and Reputation

### Do Google reviews really matter?

Yes, in two ways. Google says more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking. And many customers read reviews before they call a business they have never used.

### How do I get more reviews without being pushy?

Ask at the moment a customer is happiest, and make it easy with a direct link. Then make asking a habit after every job. Never buy reviews or offer rewards for them, because it breaks Google's rules.

Scripts and timing are in [How to Get More Google Reviews Without Breaking the Rules](/guides/get-more-google-reviews-local-business).

### What should I do about a bad review?

Reply calmly and publicly, thank them, take responsibility where it is fair, and offer to make it right offline. A thoughtful reply often earns more trust than the bad review cost you.

## Hiring Help

### How much does a marketing agency cost?

It depends on scope. Some quotes cover a few social posts, others cover your profile, reviews, website, and ads. Compare quotes line by line before comparing price.

Our [cost guide](/guides/how-much-does-it-cost-to-hire-a-marketing-agency-in-sioux-falls) explains the pricing models and what changes the cost.

### Is it a red flag if an agency guarantees a top ranking?

Yes. No agency controls Google's results. A confident agency talks about the work it will do and how it will measure results.

### Should I see results from other local businesses?

Yes. Ask for specific examples: what the problem was, what they did, and what changed. Real examples come with real detail. See [How to Choose a Marketing Agency in Sioux Falls](/guides/how-to-choose-a-marketing-agency-in-sioux-falls).

### Who should own my accounts?

You should. Your Google Business Profile, website, domain, and ad accounts should be in your business's name, with the agency added as a manager.

### Is a long contract normal?

Many local engagements run month to month. Longer terms can come with a better rate, but avoid lock-ins with no clear exit.

## Timing and Results

### How long does marketing take to work?

Ads can bring calls within days. Local search work, reviews, and content usually take months to build, because they grow over time and keep working once they do.

### When should seasonal businesses start marketing?

Before the busy season, not during it. Trades with a spring rush often use the slower winter months to build visibility and reviews. More in our [seasonal marketing guide](/guides/seasonal-marketing-sioux-falls-businesses).

### How do I know if my marketing is working?

Ask every new customer how they found you, and keep a simple tally each month. Pair it with your Google Business Profile numbers for calls, directions, and website visits.

Our guide on [how to tell if your marketing is actually working](/guides/track-marketing-results-local-business) includes a one-page scorecard.

### Where do I start if I feel behind?

Start with the pillar where you are weakest. If people cannot find you, fix visibility first. If they find you but pick someone else, work on authority and reviews. If customers never come back, build retention.
