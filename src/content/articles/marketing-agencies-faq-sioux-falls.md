## How much does local marketing cost for a small Sioux Falls business?

Typically $500-$2,500+ monthly depending on scope — local SEO-focused work runs lower, full-service marketing combining SEO, ads, and content runs higher.

## Is it a red flag if an agency guarantees a top Google ranking?

Yes — no legitimate agency controls Google's algorithm, so a specific ranking guarantee is a real warning sign rather than a sign of confidence.

## When should home service businesses start marketing before their busy season?

Well before it — since SEO and visibility building take months, many trades use the slower winter season specifically to invest, so they're positioned before spring demand hits.

## What should be included in a marketing report?

At minimum, clear metrics on visibility or leads generated, provided monthly — an agency vague about reporting cadence is worth questioning.

## Should I ask to see results from other local businesses?

Yes — real, specific examples from Sioux Falls-area clients, ideally in your industry, are more useful than general claims about experience.

## Is a long-term contract normal?

Many engagements run month-to-month; be cautious of long lock-in contracts without clear, reasonable cancellation terms.
