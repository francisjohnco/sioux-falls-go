Window cleaning seems straightforward, but second-story access and interior scheduling logistics make a company's real experience and insurance coverage worth checking before booking.

## What to verify first

- **Insurance covering ladder or lift work**, if your home has second-story windows.
- **Satisfaction guarantee policy** — a reputable company will return to fix streaks or missed spots within a reasonable window.
- **Real local reviews**, ideally recent ones mentioning similar-sized homes.

## Questions worth asking

1. **"Are you insured for second-story or ladder work specifically?"** General liability isn't always the same as coverage for elevated work.
2. **"What's included in a standard cleaning — screens, tracks, sills?"** Get this specified rather than assumed.
3. **"How do you handle a missed spot or streak after you leave?"** A clear satisfaction guarantee is a good sign.
4. **"How do you schedule around weather?"** Rain or high wind typically requires rescheduling — a professional company handles this proactively.

## Red flags

- No insurance for elevated/ladder work on a home with second-story windows
- No clear policy for addressing missed spots
- Pressure to pay in full before the job is inspected
- No local references or reviews

## Timing considerations

Spring, after winter grime and road salt residue build up, and fall, before the coldest months, are the two most common booking windows in Sioux Falls — scheduling slightly outside these peak periods can sometimes mean easier availability.
