Real estate commission in Sioux Falls, as elsewhere, is typically a percentage of the sale price, split between the listing agent's brokerage and the buyer's agent's brokerage, and is generally negotiable rather than fixed.

## How commission actually works

Total commission is commonly in the 5-6% range of sale price, though this varies and is negotiable. It's typically paid by the seller at closing and split between the listing side and buyer's side — though industry practices around buyer-agent compensation have been shifting, making this a worthwhile conversation to have directly and early with any agent.

## Costs beyond commission

- **Closing costs**, typically 2-5% of purchase price for buyers, covering items like title insurance, loan origination fees, and inspections.
- **Pre-listing prep**, if you're selling — minor repairs, staging, or professional photography, which some agents include and others quote separately.
- **Inspection-related negotiations**, which can result in seller credits or repair costs that aren't part of the agent's fee but affect your net proceeds.

## What actually affects the value you get

- **Local market knowledge specific to Sioux Falls neighborhoods**, since pricing and demand vary meaningfully between areas like downtown, McKennan Park, and the growing southeast side of the metro.
- **Marketing approach for sellers** — professional photography and how a listing is promoted can affect both time on market and final price.
- **Negotiation experience**, which is harder to quantify but matters significantly in a competitive offer situation.

## Getting clarity upfront

Ask any agent directly what's included in their commission and what, if anything, is billed separately. A clear, specific answer here is a better sign than a vague "it's all included" without detail.
