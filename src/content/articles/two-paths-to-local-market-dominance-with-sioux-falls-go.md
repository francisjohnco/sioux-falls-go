**Sioux Falls GO**  is a _community-powered marketing platform_  built to elevate the most trusted, high-rated local businesses in our city.

There are **two ways**  to enter the ecosystem:

  1. Get featured in our online directory
  2. Be invited through a Sioux Falls GO postcard

But this isn’t pay-to-play. Everything is **earned**  — through **community engagement, giving back** , and **consistently delivering excellence**.

And the best part? You don’t need a big budget to start. Simple acts like offering a **discount**  or launching a **giveaway**  create real momentum.

**Sioux Falls GO takes those small gestures and amplifies them**  — sparking excitement with local shoppers, helping you stand out, and creating real business growth.

If you’re ready to be seen, supported, and celebrated for what you bring to Sioux Falls — this guide is for you.

Let’s walk you through how to grow your visibility, build your reputation, and **claim your place in the most community-driven business platform ever built for our city.**

## The Two Ways to Enter Our Ecosystem

### 1 A. FEATURED BUSINESS

_**Your Starting Point — Visibility Rooted in Community**_

Many businesses begin their journey here. You earn your spot by doing great work — and by offering a small **discount**  or **giveaway**  to the community. That unlocks your “**Gives Back** ” badge — a powerful trust signal in the directory.

As a **Featured Business (FREE)** , you get:

  * **Visibility**  in the Sioux Falls GO Directory — so more locals discover and choose you 
  * A **“Gives Back” badge**  — showing you’re active and community-minded 
  * **Group Spotlight shoutouts**  — bringing exposure to new audiences 
  * The **Rank A Pro review app**  — making it easier to collect 5-star Google reviews 
  * A platform to build **lasting community trust**  — which leads to repeat customers and referrals

**Bottom line:**

You get real visibility, trust, and traffic — _without paying a dime._ It’s your first step toward becoming a **recognized local leader.**

### 1 B. The Sioux Falls GO Postcard

**_Handpicked for Leadership — Now It’s Time to Lead_**

If you received the **Sioux Falls GO Postcard** , it wasn’t random. You were hand-selected as a potential **ZIP Authority**  — a standout business we believe could lead in your ZIP and industry.

This postcard gives you a fast-track into our growth ecosystem with two options:

  * **Option 1: Claim Your Spot as a Featured Business**  
Launch a simple giveaway, activate your profile, and start gaining local trust and visibility right away.
  * **Option 2: Skip Ahead to Community Champion**

Already have a great reputation? You may qualify immediately for **Community Champion**  status — unlocking premium placement, campaigns, and leadership visibility.

**The postcard means you’re meant to lead.**  
This is your moment to engage, contribute, and grow your local authority.

## Level Up with Next Growth Opportunity 

### 2\. Community Campions 

**_You’ve got visibility — now it’s time to lead._**

**Community Champions**  
Community champs is here good businesses become local icons; it's where businesses consistently step up and deliver great service, support local causes, share their stories, and engage with purpose.

As a Champion, you unlock:

  * A **Premium Business Page**  — boosting trust and conversions
  * A **Window Sticker & Online Badge** — building instant credibility
  * **Exclusive Spotlight Campaigns**  — reaching thousands in your ZIP
  * **Top-ranked placement**  in your category
  * A feature in a **high-authority article or guide**  — building brand equity and SEO

**To qualify:**

• Maintain **4.5+ star reviews**  (Google or similar)

• Actively participate with Sioux Falls GO (content, giveaways, updates)

• Align with our **community-first mission and values**

This is where visibility turns into **reputation, leadership, and revenue.**

## **ZIP Authority**

**One ZIP. One Industry. One Leader.**

**ZIP Authority**  is our _most exclusive_  opportunity.

Only **one business per ZIP per industry**  can hold this title — and once claimed, it’s locked unless that business steps away.

This status can’t be bought. It must be **earned**  — through trust, consistency, and proven community leadership.

To qualify, your business must:

• Maintain **strong, consistent customer satisfaction**

• Actively **collaborate**  with Sioux Falls GO (content, backlinks, giveaways)

• Show a clear **commitment to local impact**

• Be ready to grow **with and for your ZIP**

When your ZIP is claimed — it’s yours.

That’s why **timing and alignment matter.**

_Note: ZIP Authority benefits are still expanding — expect exclusive exposure, insights, and growth opportunities that no one else in your ZIP will have._
