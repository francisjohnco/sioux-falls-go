Sioux Falls retail centers around two major hubs, but neighborhood-level options are genuinely worth knowing too.

## The Empire Mall district

The city's largest concentrated retail area, drawing shoppers from across the broader region for mainstream and big-box needs.

## Downtown

A genuinely distinct independent retail scene, benefiting from the same ongoing revitalization that's shaped the area's dining and coffee shop growth.

## The growing southeast side

As one of Sioux Falls's fastest-growing areas, the southeast side has seen real retail development alongside its residential expansion — an increasingly practical option for residents in that part of the metro rather than always defaulting to the mall district or downtown.

## Why it's worth knowing your own area's options

Sioux Falls's growth means retail options increasingly exist closer to home than they did even a few years ago — worth checking what's actually nearby before defaulting to a longer trip out of habit.
