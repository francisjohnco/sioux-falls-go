Every so often, a local business earns more than a spot in the directory — this month, that's SAJMedia Marketing.

As a Community Champion, SAJMedia has gone beyond meeting the bar for a Sioux Falls Go listing. They've invested directly back into the platform, helping fund the giveaways and community spotlights every resident sees.

We sat down with Francis, who leads the agency, to talk about what's changed for local businesses trying to get found online — and why he thinks marketing should feel like clarity, not chaos.
