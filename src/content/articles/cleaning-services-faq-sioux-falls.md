## How much does recurring house cleaning cost?

Typically $100-$200 per visit for an average home on a bi-weekly or monthly schedule, with one-time deep cleans usually priced higher.

## Should cleaners be bonded, not just insured?

Yes — bonding specifically covers theft, which standard insurance doesn't, and matters given cleaners often work in your home unsupervised.

## Why does winter seem to create more cleaning work?

Road salt and de-icing chemicals tracked in on shoes create residue that builds up on entryway flooring faster than typical dirt, and dry indoor air increases static-driven dust buildup — both real seasonal patterns here.

## Do I need to be home while my house is cleaned?

Not necessarily, though being present for a first visit with a new company is a reasonable way to build trust before leaving them unsupervised going forward.

## Is a deep clean worth it before starting recurring service?

Often yes, especially if your home hasn't had regular professional cleaning — it addresses existing buildup so the recurring schedule can maintain rather than catch up.

## Do cleaning companies bring their own supplies?

Most do, but confirm upfront — some offer a discount if you supply your own products, which also matters if you have specific allergy or preference concerns.
