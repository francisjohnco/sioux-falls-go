Residential window cleaning in Sioux Falls typically runs $4-$8 per pane for standard windows, with most whole-home jobs falling in the $150-$350 range depending on window count and whether both interior and exterior are included.

## Typical price ranges

- **Exterior only, average home:** $150-$250
- **Interior and exterior, average home:** $250-$400
- **Screen cleaning (add-on):** $2-$5 per screen
- **Track and sill detailing (add-on):** often $1-$3 per window

## What actually moves the price

- **Window count and size.** Larger or unusually shaped windows (bay windows, large picture windows) can take more time per pane.
- **Accessibility.** Second-story or hard-to-reach windows may require ladders or special equipment, adding to cost.
- **Interior access.** Coordinating around furniture, blinds, and window treatments adds time compared to exterior-only service.

## Seasonal timing

Many Sioux Falls homeowners schedule window cleaning in spring after winter grime and salt residue accumulate, and again in fall before the coldest months set in. Booking outside the busiest spring rush can sometimes mean better scheduling flexibility.

## Getting a fair quote

Ask whether screens and track cleaning are included or separate — a quote that seems notably cheaper than others may simply exclude these add-ons rather than being a genuinely better deal for the same scope of work.
