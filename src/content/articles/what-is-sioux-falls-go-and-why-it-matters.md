_A new way to support local—and build a stronger Sioux Falls._

**What Is Sioux Falls Go and How It Works for Our Community**

Sioux Falls Go is a community powered platform that connects locals with trusted local businesses and makes supporting Sioux Falls fun and rewarding.

Whether you are a resident looking for reliable services or a business wanting to grow your visibility, Sioux Falls Go brings the community together in a powerful new way.

We highlight businesses that serve Sioux Falls with excellence, give back in meaningful ways, and make this city a better place for everyone who lives here.

This is the guide to how it works and how you can be part of it.

## **For Locals**

**A Better Way to Discover Great Local Businesses**

The Sioux Falls Go directory makes it easy to find businesses that care about the community. Every business in our directory participates in at least one community giveaway, offers a local incentive, or engages in local support. You are not just choosing a business. You are choosing people who show up for Sioux Falls.

Look for three badges inside the directory:

  * **Gives Back  
** Businesses that participate in at least one community giveaway or offer a local only special.
  * **Community Champion  
** Businesses that show consistent excellence, strong service, and meaningful community engagement.
  * **City Authority  
** Leaders in their field who demonstrate expertise, integrity, and a clear track record of serving Sioux Falls well.

These badges help locals find the most trusted businesses in the city.

### **Why Locals Love Sioux Falls Go**

**1\. Giveaways and Rewards**  
Supporting local should be fun. We run regular giveaways so locals can win prizes while discovering new businesses.

**2\. Trusted Recommendations**  
You never waste time guessing. Our directory highlights businesses that have earned community trust.

**3\. Easy to Use**  
Explore by category, read spotlights, and choose businesses that give back to Sioux Falls.

**4\. Community Impact**  
Every choice supports local jobs, families, and dreams. When you support these businesses, you help the entire city grow.

## **For Businesses**

**A Platform Built to Help You Grow**

Sioux Falls Go is designed to help high quality local businesses gain visibility and connect with more customers. You do not need a big marketing budget. You just need a commitment to serving the community well.

### **How Businesses Join**

You enter the directory by:

  * Scheduling a spotlight call
  * Sharing your business story
  * Offering one giveaway or local incentive
  * Allowing us to feature you in the community

Once you are in, your business receives:

  * A full directory listing
  * A Gives Back banner
  * Exposure in group spotlights
  * Access to our community driven marketing system

This is completely free.

### **The Recognition Levels**

Every business starts at the same point and can grow through earned recognition.

**1\. Gives Back**  
Your first step. One giveaway or local incentive unlocks your badge and helps locals find you.

**2\. Community Champion**  
Awarded to businesses with strong service, great reputation, and consistent community engagement. Champions receive:

  * Priority visibility
  * A window sticker
  * Spotlight campaigns
  * Editorial features in guides and lists

**3\. City Authority**  
A distinction for leaders in their field. These businesses demonstrate mastery, professionalism, integrity, and city wide trust. They may publish educational content on Sioux Falls Go to help locals make informed choices.

This recognition reflects excellence, not advertising power.

### **Why Businesses Love Sioux Falls Go**

**1\. Real Visibility**  
You are seen by thousands of locals who value community focused businesses.

**2\. Fast Trust**  
Badges help people immediately understand who you are and how you show up for Sioux Falls.

**3\. Free Entry**  
The system is free for businesses. You simply give back to the community through one small act.

**4\. SEO and Reputation Growth**  
Spotlights, articles, and community mentions help strengthen your online presence.

**5\. Community Powered Marketing**  
Word of mouth spreads faster when the whole city participates.

## **How Sioux Falls Go Helps the Whole City**

Sioux Falls grows stronger when:

  * Locals find businesses they can trust
  * Businesses feel supported
  * The community has fun reasons to participate
  * Giving back becomes normal
  * Commerce builds connection

This is more than a directory. It is a movement toward a more connected, generous, locally focused Sioux Falls.
