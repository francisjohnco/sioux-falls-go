Sioux Falls is one of the fastest-growing cities in the country, which means a lot of people reading this just moved here, or are about to. Here's a starting point that skips the generic relocation-guide advice and points you toward things that are actually real and current.

## Get oriented downtown first

Nearly everything in Sioux Falls radiates outward from Phillips Avenue and [Falls Park](/businesses/falls-park), the waterfall the city is named for. Spend an afternoon walking Phillips Avenue past the historic quartzite buildings, and you'll pass most of downtown's restaurants, the [SculptureWalk](/businesses/sculpturewalk) outdoor art exhibit, and a genuine sense of where things are relative to each other. It's the single best hour you can spend to stop feeling lost in a new city.

## Pick a neighborhood that fits how you live

If you're choosing where to live, our [neighborhood guides](/neighborhoods/downtown) cover the areas people actually talk about. The [Cathedral District](/neighborhoods/cathedral-district) and [McKennan Park](/neighborhoods/mckennan-park) both offer walkable, historic character close to downtown. [Whittier](/neighborhoods/whittier) is a quieter, more residential option that still keeps you close to downtown employers.

## Find local businesses you can actually trust

The rest of this site exists for exactly this. Every business listed here has a real, checked rating, not just whoever paid for a listing. Start with our [restaurant](/restaurants-dining) and [home services](/home-services) directories when you need a place to eat or someone to fix something, and check the neighborhood pages for what's genuinely nearby before you commit to a gym, salon, or coffee shop three miles across town.

## Know what's coming up

Sioux Falls runs on a real seasonal rhythm — farmers markets and free outdoor concerts all summer, Oktoberfests and craft markets every fall. Our [events page](/events) tracks what's actually happening in the weeks ahead, not a stale list from last year.

## The short version

Walk downtown first. Pick a neighborhood based on what you actually want, not just what shows up first in a search. And bookmark this site for anything you need done around the house or a place to eat — everything here has been checked, not just listed.
