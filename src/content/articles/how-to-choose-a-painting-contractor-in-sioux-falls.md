South Dakota doesn't require a state license for painting contractors, which shifts more of the verification burden onto you as the homeowner — insurance and reputation matter more here than they might for a licensed trade.

## What to verify first

- **Liability insurance.** Ask for proof directly. This protects your property if something goes wrong — a ladder through a window, paint on landscaping — during the job.
- **Real local reviews and reputation**, not just a portfolio of photos. Ask how long they've worked in the Sioux Falls area specifically.
- **A written scope of work**, not just a price. It should specify surface prep, number of coats, and paint brand/quality tier.

## Questions worth asking

1. **"What prep work is included, specifically?"** Scraping, sanding, and priming matter more to the final result than the paint brand itself.
2. **"How do you handle weather delays on exterior jobs?"** A Sioux Falls summer can bring sudden storms — ask how that affects your timeline and whether it affects price.
3. **"Do you warranty the work, and for how long?"** Standard warranties on labor are common; ask what voids it.
4. **"Can I see examples of work you've done in the last year?"** Recent local work tells you more than an old portfolio.

## Red flags

- No insurance, or reluctance to provide proof
- A bid dramatically lower than others with no explanation of what's excluded
- Full payment requested before any work starts
- No written scope of work, just a verbal price

## The Sioux Falls timing factor

Because the exterior painting season here is genuinely shorter than in milder climates, contractors with strong local reputations tend to book up early in the season. If you're planning exterior work, reaching out in early spring — even for a summer or fall booking — is worth doing rather than waiting until you need it done immediately.
