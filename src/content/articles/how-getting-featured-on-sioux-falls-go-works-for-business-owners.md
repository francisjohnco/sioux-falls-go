Sioux Falls Go was built to spotlight the businesses that are truly making a difference in our community. Not through ads. Not through big budgets. But through real service, real excellence, and real heart.

Because of that, our process is a little different than a typical “sign up and pay” directory. It is personal, intentional, and built around connection. Here is exactly how it works.

**Step 1: How We Find Businesses**

We do not wait for random signups. Most of the businesses we feature are found through the community.

Here is how that happens:

  * We watch local Facebook groups
  * We read comment threads on recommendation posts
  * We pay attention when people ask “Who do you recommend for…?”
  * We listen to what locals consistently praise

If your business is being talked about for the right reasons, that gets our attention.

We also search for hidden gems. Businesses that may not be loud online but quietly serve the city with excellence every day.

That is how you get on our radar.

### Step 2: Your Invitation

If we believe your business aligns with our mission, we personally reach out and invite you.

This is important to understand: You are never automatically listed. You are personally invited. Because Sioux Falls Go is invite-only, not pay-to-play.

We spotlight local legends who serve with care, integrity, and consistency. If you are interested, then and only then do we move to the next step.

### Step 3: Why We Ask You to Book a Call

This is where many people hesitate, so let’s clear this up.

The call is not a sales call. The call is not a pitch. The call is not you being sold anything. The call exists for one reason:

So we can tell your story the right way.

We do not create generic business listings. We create community-centered spotlights. That means we need:

  * Your story
  * Your heart behind the business
  * Why you started
  * Who you serve
  * What makes your work meaningful

This is what turns your page from a basic business profile into something people actually connect with.

**The call is only 15 minutes.**

Just a simple, friendly conversation so we can introduce you properly to the city.

### Step 4: We Build Your Spotlight Page

After your 15-minute interview, we take everything you shared and craft your spotlight profile.

This page:

  * Introduces you to the community
  * Highlights your values and story
  * Builds trust before people even call you
  * Sets you apart from generic listings

### Step 5: You Review Everything Before We Publish

We do not publish anything without your approval. We schedule a short **30-minute review call** where:

  * You see your full page
  * You make any changes you want
  * We fine-tune wording together
  * We ensure it fully represents you and your business

Only after you approve it do we publish it live.

### Step 6: We Help You Build Local Authority With Reviews

Once your page is live, we also help you strengthen your online authority.

You will receive access to the **Ranker Pro app**.

This makes it easy for you to:

  * Send review links to customers
  * Get more 5-star Google reviews
  * Build stronger social proof
  * Increase trust in the community

We walk you through exactly how to use it on your onboarding call.

## **Why We Do It This Way**

We do not believe businesses should have to spend thousands of dollars on ads just to be seen.

We believe businesses should be seen because:

  * They serve well
  * They care about people
  * They give back
  * They earned the community’s trust

The call simply allows us to honor your story correctly.

## **The Whole Process Is Simple**

Here is the full flow in one glance:

  1. The community talks about your business
  2. We invite you personally
  3. You book a 15-minute story call
  4. We build your spotlight page
  5. You review and approve it
  6. We publish it
  7. We help you grow with visibility and reviews

That is it.

No pressure.

No complicated onboarding.

No hidden traps.

Just real community connection.
