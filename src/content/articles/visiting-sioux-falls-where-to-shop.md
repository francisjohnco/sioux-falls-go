If you're visiting Sioux Falls and have some time for shopping alongside sightseeing, downtown offers something more memorable than a standard mall trip.

## Downtown, alongside your sightseeing

Downtown's independent boutiques and specialty shops sit within the same walkable core as Falls Park and the SculptureWalk, making it easy to fold shopping into a day of exploring rather than treating it as a separate errand.

## If you want something distinctly Sioux Falls

Downtown's independent retailers are where you'll find gifts and finds with real local character, distinct from what's available at the same national retailers you could visit anywhere.

## If you need something practical

The Empire Mall district covers familiar, efficient retail needs if you need something specific and don't have time for a more exploratory downtown visit.

## A simple approach

If your visit includes downtown sightseeing, build in time to browse a shop or two while you're already there — it's a low-effort way to bring home something more meaningful than a standard souvenir.
