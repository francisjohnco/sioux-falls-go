South Dakota licenses plumbers at the state level, which means the first and easiest check you can do is confirm the person showing up at your door is actually licensed to do the work, not just employed by a company that has a license somewhere on file.

## Start here: verify, don't assume

Ask directly for a license number and, if you want to be thorough, you can call the state board to confirm it's current. A plumber who hesitates or gets defensive about this question is telling you something. A plumber who has the number memorized and offers it before you ask is telling you something too.

Beyond licensing, confirm:

- **Insurance and bonding.** This protects you if something goes wrong in your home during the job, not just the plumber.
- **Whether they pull permits when the job requires one.** The City of Sioux Falls Building Services office (231 N. Dakota Avenue) requires permits for a range of plumbing work — a plumber who routinely skips this to save time is cutting a corner that becomes your problem at resale, not theirs.

## Questions worth asking before you book

1. **"Is this a flat quote, or could it change once you see the pipe?"** Answered above in our cost guide — but it's worth asking directly, because the honest answer is often "it depends," and you want to hear how they explain that.
2. **"Do you warranty your work, and for how long?"** A standard warranty on labor is common; if there isn't one, ask why.
3. **"Have you worked on homes in [my neighborhood] before?"** Older Sioux Falls neighborhoods often have original galvanized plumbing that behaves differently than modern PEX systems — experience with your home's era matters more than it might seem.
4. **"What's your response time for an actual emergency?"** Get a real answer, not "as soon as we can." A burst pipe at 11pm in February isn't a hypothetical here.

## Red flags

- Refusing to provide a license number or insurance certificate
- A quote that's dramatically lower than every other estimate you got, with no explanation for the gap
- Pressure to sign or pay before the scope of work is written down anywhere
- No physical business address you can find — a truck and a phone number aren't the same as an established local business

## The trust shortcut

A Sioux Falls Go listing already means a plumber has cleared our own bar for reputation and community involvement before they're recommended here — that's a reasonable starting point, but the questions above are still worth asking directly, for any plumber, anywhere you find them.
