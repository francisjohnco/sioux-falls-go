Hiring a marketing agency is harder to judge than hiring almost any other help. A plumber either fixes the leak or doesn't. Marketing results take months to show, and by then you have already paid.

That is why the choice deserves real care. This guide gives you a clear way to compare agencies, the questions to ask in the first meeting, and the warning signs that should send you elsewhere.

## Start With What You Actually Need

Before you call anyone, get clear on your biggest problem. It makes every conversation sharper.

Most local businesses are weakest in one of four areas, which we explain in [The 4 Pillars of a Growing Sioux Falls Business](/guides/four-pillars-local-business-growth-sioux-falls).

- **Not getting found?** You need visibility help: your Google Business Profile, local search, and listings.
- **Getting found but losing to competitors?** You need authority: a stronger website, service pages, and real proof of your work.
- **Thin or mixed reviews?** You need reputation help: a system for earning reviews and replying to them.
- **Few repeat customers?** You need retention: follow up, reminders, and referrals.

An agency that is great at social media may not be the right fit if your real gap is visibility. Knowing your gap keeps you from buying the wrong service.

## Look for Proof, Not Promises

Every agency says it gets results. The good ones can show you.

**Ask for specific examples.** Which local businesses have they helped, what was the problem, what did they do, and what changed? Real examples come with detail.

**Look at their own presence.** Does their Google Business Profile have steady, recent reviews? Is their own website clear and useful? An agency that cannot market itself well is a warning sign.

**Talk to a current client.** A short call with someone they work with now tells you more than any sales deck.

## Questions to Ask in the First Meeting

1. **"What would you work on first for my business, and why?"** A good answer is specific to you, not a package.
2. **"What exactly is included each month, and what costs extra?"** Get it in writing.
3. **"What should be different in 90 days?"** Listen for realistic, measurable changes.
4. **"How will you report results, and how often?"** Reports should connect to calls, leads, and customers.
5. **"Who will actually do the work on my account?"** Meet that person if you can.
6. **"Who owns my accounts, website, and content?"** The answer should be you.
7. **"What happens if I want to cancel?"** Know the notice period before you sign.

Our [cost guide](/guides/how-much-does-it-cost-to-hire-a-marketing-agency-in-sioux-falls) explains how agencies price their work, so you can compare answers to questions 2 and 7 fairly.

## Red Flags

- **Guaranteed rankings.** Google says there is no way to pay for a better local ranking. No agency controls it.
- **Secret methods.** Good marketing is not a secret. If they cannot explain what they will do, be careful.
- **Vague scope or blended pricing**, especially ad spend mixed in with management fees.
- **Reports that never mention customers**, only impressions, followers, or likes.
- **They keep your accounts in their name.** Your profile, website, and ad accounts should be yours.
- **Pressure to sign today.** A good partner is happy to let you think it over.
- **Fake or bought reviews**, for them or offered for you. It breaks Google's rules and puts your business at risk.

## Why Local Experience Helps

Sioux Falls is growing fast. The city added 5,088 residents in 2025 alone and reached 224,676 people, according to figures shared by Mayor Paul TenHaken and reported by SiouxFalls.Business in January 2026.

An agency that knows the city understands how word of mouth and online search work together here. They know the neighborhoods, the suburbs, the seasons that drive your demand, and the community events where local businesses get noticed.

That does not mean an out-of-town agency cannot do good work. It means local knowledge is a real advantage, and worth asking about.

## Make the Decision

Narrow it to two or three agencies. Compare them on four things:

- **Fit.** Do they focus on your biggest gap?
- **Proof.** Did they show real, specific results?
- **Clarity.** Do you understand exactly what you get and what it costs?
- **Trust.** Did they answer honestly, including about what they cannot promise?

Choose the one that scores well on all four, not just the cheapest or the slickest pitch. Then agree on what success looks like in 90 days, and check in on it together.
