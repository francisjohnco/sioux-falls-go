Marketing agencies sell a service that's harder to evaluate upfront than most home services — the actual quality only becomes clear over months, which makes checking real track record more important here than almost anywhere else.

## What to check first

- **Real, specific examples of past local results**, not just generic claims about experience.
- **Clear scope of what's actually included** month to month, in writing.
- **Reporting cadence and transparency** — how you'll actually know if the work is producing results.

## Questions worth asking

1. **"Can you show me results for another Sioux Falls business, ideally in my industry?"** Specific examples matter more than general claims.
2. **"What exactly is included each month, and what would cost extra?"** Get this in writing before signing anything.
3. **"How and how often will I see reporting on results?"** Monthly reporting at minimum is a reasonable standard.
4. **"What happens if I want to cancel — what's the notice period?"** Understand exit terms before you're locked into a relationship that isn't working.

## Red flags

- Guaranteeing a specific search ranking position — no legitimate agency controls Google's algorithm
- Vague or evasive answers about what's included in the monthly cost
- No willingness to share real local client results
- Long contract lock-in with unclear cancellation terms

## Why local experience matters

An agency with real experience marketing Sioux Falls businesses understands local search patterns, local competition, and what actually resonates with this specific community — genuinely different from a marketing approach built for a national or generic audience.
