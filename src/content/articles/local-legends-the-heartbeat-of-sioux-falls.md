In every thriving city, there’s an unsung group of heroes — people who believe in more than just good prices or convenience. 

They believe in their neighbors. In local jobs. In building a community that stands tall and looks out for one another.

In Sioux Falls, we call them **Local Legends**.

## **Who Are Local Legends?**

Local Legends are everyday Sioux Falls shoppers who make extraordinary choices. They choose to hire local, shop local, eat local — because they understand that _every dollar spent locally is a vote for the future of our city_.

When you support a hometown business, you’re helping a young entrepreneur keep their dream alive, keeping families employed, and ensuring that businesses that give back to the community can continue to do so.

Local Legends are more than customers — they are investors in our culture, our economy, and our connection to one another. They don’t just “buy things.” They build things. And what they build is worth honoring.

> **Local Legends make Sioux Falls stronger, more connected, and more vibrant with every choice they make.**

## **Inside the Circle: Sioux Falls GO VIP Shoppers**

Among the Local Legends is a growing circle of shoppers who go even further — we call them **Sioux Falls GO VIP Shoppers**. They’re not just passionate about shopping local… they’ve turned it into a mission.

**VIP Shoppers**  get early access to promotions, earn exclusive perks, and help steer the future of local commerce by nominating standout businesses, voting on local rankings, and being part of our Local Legends community.

## **Why become a VIP?**

• **Early Access to Giveaways & Events**  
Be the first to hear about limited-time offers, shop local events, and exclusive promotions from top-tier businesses.

• **Members-Only Discounts**  
Get special deals from businesses that display the _Community Champion_ , _Zip Authority_ , or _Gives Back_  badges — only visible to local-first supporters like you.

• **Influence the Future of Sioux Falls**  
Nominate your favorite businesses for features, campaigns, and awards. You’re not just a shopper — you’re a voice that matters.

## **The Symbols That Matter: What to Look For**

To spot a Local-Legend-approved business, keep an eye out for these meaningful badges on Sioux Falls GO:

• **Gives Back Banner**  – This means the business participates in giveaways and offers discounts to locals to create engagement.

• **Community Champion Badge**  – These businesses don’t just operate locally, they advocate for community causes and go above and beyond to serve Sioux Falls.

• **ZIP Authority**  – An elite badge earned by select community champion businesses for excellence. Only one business per industry, per ZIP code — representing the gold standard of local leadership, service, and impact.

Because shopping locally isn’t just a transaction. It’s a transformation.

It transforms strangers into neighbors. It turns one small purchase into a ripple effect that strengthens jobs, schools, services, and the overall spirit of Sioux Falls.
