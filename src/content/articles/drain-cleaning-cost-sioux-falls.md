Most people who search for drain cleaning prices are standing in an inch of water or staring at a sink that will not drain, so here is the short version. A single clogged fixture usually costs a couple hundred dollars to clear. A clogged main sewer line costs more, and a job that needs hydro jetting or a camera inspection can run into the high hundreds or beyond. What you actually pay depends on where the clog is, how it gets cleared, and when you call. If you are still working out what kind of clog you have, start with our [complete guide to drain cleaning in Sioux Falls](/guides/drain-cleaning-sioux-falls).

One honest note before the numbers. The ranges below are national figures from consumer cost guides, not Sioux Falls averages. Local companies set their own rates, and published guides disagree with each other by a wide margin. Use these numbers to spot a quote that is far out of line, not as a promise of what any company will charge.

## What Drain Cleaning Typically Costs

Here are the ranges most national guides land on for a standard residential visit during business hours.

| Job | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Kitchen sink | $100 to $250 |
| Toilet | $100 to $300 |
| Bathtub or shower | $150 to $300 |
| Main sewer line, snaking | $150 to $800 |
| Main sewer line, hydro jetting | $350 to $1,000, more with a camera or a long root-filled line |
| Camera inspection | $150 to $500 at many companies, more for a thorough recorded report |

The single fixture figures follow [Angi](https://match.angi.com/cost/plumbing/repair-a-clogged-drain), and the camera inspection range stretches toward the top of what [HomeAdvisor](https://homeadvisor.com/cost/plumbing/repair-a-clogged-drain) reports for thorough work. Main line and jetting ranges vary the most between sources, which tells you something. Anyone who gives you one flat number over the phone for a main line, without knowing what is in it, is guessing.

## What Pushes the Price Up in Sioux Falls

Where the clog sits matters most. A clog in the trap under one sink is a short job. A clog in the main line that carries everything out of the house needs a longer cable or a jetter, access through a cleanout, and more time.

The cause matters too. Hair and soap are easy. Grease that has hardened inside a kitchen line, and roots that have pushed into an older sewer pipe, are not. Older neighborhoods with mature trees are the classic setup for root problems, because roots go looking for the moisture inside aging clay or cast iron pipe.

Sioux Falls water adds a slow problem of its own. Water drawn from the Big Sioux Aquifer is hard, roughly 12.8 to 15 grains per gallon, and hard water leaves mineral scale on the inside of pipes over the years. Scale narrows a line and gives grease and hair something to catch on, which is one reason a drain that was cleared can slow down again.

Timing changes the bill as well. Nights, weekends, and holidays usually cost more than a weekday appointment, and a sewer backup during the spring thaw tends to happen when every company is busy.

## How to Get a Fair Quote in Sioux Falls

A few plain questions protect you from most surprises.

- What does the visit fee cover, and is it credited toward the work?
- Is the price flat or by the hour, and what happens if the first method does not clear it?
- Will you run a camera if this is a main line problem or a repeat clog?
- Who is coming out, and are they a licensed plumber? South Dakota licenses plumbers at the state level, and a licensed plumber will not mind being asked.
- Can I have the price in writing before you start?

For anything more than a routine single clog, get a second quote. ## When the Cheaper Option Costs More Later

A low price for clearing a drain is a good deal only if the drain stays clear. A cable can punch a hole through a grease clog and leave the pipe walls coated, so the line slows down again within weeks. Roots grow back. Scale does not go away on its own.

If the same drain has clogged more than once in a few months, paying for a camera look is usually cheaper than paying for a third quick clear. It shows whether you have grease, roots, scale, or a damaged section, and it gives you something concrete to compare between quotes.
