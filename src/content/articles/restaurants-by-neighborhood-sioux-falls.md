While downtown holds real concentration, Sioux Falls's neighborhoods each have their own dining character worth knowing.

## Downtown and Phillips Avenue

The highest concentration and variety, benefiting from ongoing revitalization and steady foot traffic from workers, residents, and visitors alike.

## McKennan Park and All Saints

These established, walkable neighborhoods have their own smaller but genuine collection of local favorites, often with the kind of regulars' culture that develops in a stable residential area.

## The southeast side

As one of Sioux Falls's fastest-growing areas, the southeast side has seen real restaurant growth alongside its residential expansion, serving a newer population with its own developing dining identity.

## Why exploring beyond your own neighborhood is worth it

Sioux Falls is compact enough that a twenty-minute drive can put you in a genuinely different part of the dining scene — worth doing periodically rather than only ever eating within your immediate area.
