Sioux Falls windows take real seasonal wear that's worth understanding before assuming a single annual cleaning is enough.

## Why winter is hard on windows

Road salt spray from passing traffic, sand from de-icing efforts, and general winter grime build up on exterior glass over the cold months, often more noticeably than typical dust accumulation in other seasons. This is part of why many Sioux Falls homeowners see a real difference after a proper spring cleaning, not just a cosmetic touch-up.

## Why cleaning in freezing temperatures doesn't work well

Cleaning solution can freeze on glass surfaces before it's properly wiped away in genuinely cold conditions, leading to streaking rather than a clean result. Most professional window cleaners prefer scheduling when temperatures are consistently above freezing, which naturally pushes exterior work toward spring through fall.

## The case for cleaning twice a year

- **Spring cleaning** addresses the accumulated winter grime and salt residue, restoring clarity after the toughest season.
- **Fall cleaning**, before the coldest months set in, maximizes natural light through your windows during Sioux Falls's shorter winter days — genuinely useful when daylight is already limited.

## Maximizing winter daylight

With Sioux Falls winter days offering noticeably less daylight than summer, clean windows make a real difference in how much natural light actually reaches your home's interior. A fall cleaning, timed before the season's low-light months, is a small step that meaningfully affects daily comfort through winter.
