## How is home watch different from a house sitter?

Home watch is a professional oversight service with structured, documented visits checking specific systems and conditions — not casual visits. It typically includes active management of things like mail flow, lighting, and exterior appearance, rather than just "checking in."

## How often should my home be checked in winter?

Weekly is commonly recommended during peak winter months, given how quickly a frozen pipe or heating failure can cause serious damage in a vacant home.

## What temperature should I set my thermostat to while away?

Many professionals recommend no lower than 55-60°F, balancing freeze protection against unnecessary heating costs over a long absence.

## Does home watch include emergency response?

Often to some degree, but confirm specifics — some companies include a certain amount of emergency response in standard pricing, others bill it separately.

## How much does home watch service cost?

Typically $30-$75 per visit, or $150-$300 monthly for regular weekly checks, with seasonal snowbird packages often priced as a season-long bundle.

## Should home watch providers be certified?

Certification through the National Home Watch Association indicates defined training and insurance standards, which is a meaningful signal, though experienced uncertified providers can also do excellent work.
