## Why do appliances seem to wear out faster in Sioux Falls?

Hard water, commonly in the 12.8-15 grains per gallon range here, accelerates scale buildup in water heaters, dishwashers, and washing machines, which is a real reason these appliances often need service sooner than in areas with softer water.

## How much does a typical appliance repair service call cost?

The diagnostic visit typically runs $75-$150, with total repair cost depending on the specific appliance and part needed.

## Should I repair or replace an older appliance?

A common guideline is that if repair costs exceed roughly half of replacement cost, or the appliance is near its typical lifespan, replacement often makes more financial sense — a trustworthy technician will tell you this honestly.

## Does winter affect appliance performance?

Yes — water heaters work harder due to colder incoming water, and washers/dishwashers can see reduced performance from the combination of cold water and hard water scale buildup.

## Should I install a water softener to protect my appliances?

Many Sioux Falls homeowners do, specifically to extend the lifespan of water heaters and other water-using appliances given how hard local water runs.

## Is a warranty on appliance repair work standard?

Yes, most reputable companies warranty both parts and labor for a defined period — ask specifically what's covered before agreeing to the repair.
