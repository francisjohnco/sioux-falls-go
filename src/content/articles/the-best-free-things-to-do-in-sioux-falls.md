**The Best Free Things to Do in Sioux Falls**

_Explore Big Fun on a Small Budget_

Sioux Falls isn’t just a growing city—it’s a **hidden playground** for locals and visitors who know where to look. Whether you’re exploring for the first time or you’ve called this city home for decades, there’s something magical about discovering just how much **you can do for free**.

No tickets. No entry fees. No stress.

Just fresh air, rich culture, and unforgettable moments.

Below is your **ultimate guide** to the best free things to do in Sioux Falls—curated to **retire every generic “Top 10” list** out there. Bookmark this. Share it. Let’s explore like legends.

### **1\. Wander Through Falls Park**

Let’s start with the crown jewel.

Falls Park is the heartbeat of Sioux Falls—**123 acres** of scenic walking trails, observation towers, historic ruins, and the iconic triple waterfall that gave our city its name.

• Bring a picnic and enjoy sunset by the falls

• Watch the lights dance on the water at night

• Snap Instagram-worthy shots from the five-story tower

**Pro Tip:** Visit in winter for the **Falls Park Winter Wonderland** —a holiday light display that’s pure magic.

### **2\. Walk the SculptureWalk in Downtown**

Art should be everywhere—and in Sioux Falls, it is.

**SculptureWalk** is a rotating outdoor art exhibit that stretches through Downtown, featuring dozens of sculptures from local and international artists.

• Family-friendly scavenger hunt opportunity

• Changes every year—always something new

• Bonus: Many shops downtown participate in Sioux Falls Go giveaways and deals. Look for “Gives Back” stickers!

### **3\. Tour the Historic Cathedral District**

Step into history with a self-guided stroll through the **Cathedral Historic District** , where timeless architecture and storytelling meet.

• Don’t miss **St. Joseph Cathedral** , one of the most stunning churches in the region

• Nearby, explore the **Old Courthouse Museum** (also free) for a deeper dive into Sioux Falls’ past

### **4\. Attend a Free Outdoor Concert or Movie**

Keep an eye on community calendars—Sioux Falls is known for its **free entertainment under the stars**.

• **Levitt at the Falls** hosts dozens of free concerts all summer

• **Moonlight Movies** at Fawick Park bring family classics to life

• Many events are sponsored by **local Guardian businesses** that support the community through Sioux Falls Go

### **5\. Bike or Walk the Big Sioux River Greenway**

Stretching over **34 miles** , the Greenway is the city’s best-kept secret for peaceful connection with nature.

• Start near Cherry Rock Park and make your way toward Falls Park

• Great for bikes, rollerblades, or a leisurely afternoon walk

• Keep your eyes peeled for local wildlife—it’s more active than you think

### **6\. Enjoy the Visual Arts Center (Inside Washington Pavilion)**

While some areas of the Pavilion require paid tickets, the **Visual Arts Center** is open to the public and completely free.

• Rotating exhibits highlight local, regional, and national talent

• A perfect indoor activity for colder months

• Bonus: Walk through the atrium—it often hosts pop-up events and local showcases

### **7\. Relax at Terrace Park’s Japanese Gardens**

Tucked away in the heart of the city, the **Japanese Gardens at Terrace Park** are like a scene from a storybook.

• Peaceful paths with koi ponds, stone lanterns, and blooming trees

• Perfect for engagement photos or quiet reflection

• Don’t forget to explore the rest of Terrace Park—it’s a treasure trove of open space

### **8\. Experience Art at the First Friday Art Walk**

Every first Friday of the month, Downtown Sioux Falls transforms into an art gallery.

• Local businesses host pop-up shows

• Musicians and street performers line Phillips Ave

• Grab a bite from a participating Community Champion restaurant offering Local Legend VIP discounts

### **9\. Step Into Sioux Falls History**

Learn something new at one of these **free museums** :

• **Old Courthouse Museum** – Three floors of fascinating local history

• **Pettigrew Home & Museum** – Step inside the 1889 home of South Dakota’s first senator

• **The Veteran’s Memorial Park** – A moving outdoor space honoring local service members

### **10\. Discover Sioux Falls Murals and Hidden Alleys**

Downtown Sioux Falls is filled with colorful surprises.

• Find the “You Are Loved” mural near 8th & Railroad

• Check out the alley behind CH Patisserie for creative gems

• Many are created or sponsored by local artists and small businesses—support them when you can!
