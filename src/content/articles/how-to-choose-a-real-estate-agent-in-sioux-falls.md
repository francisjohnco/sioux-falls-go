Sioux Falls's real estate market has real neighborhood-level variation — downtown, McKennan Park, and the fast-growing southeast side each have different pricing dynamics — which makes an agent's specific local experience genuinely relevant, not just a nice-to-have.

## What to check first

- **Active South Dakota real estate license**, confirmable through the state licensing board if you want to verify directly.
- **Recent transactions in your specific area**, not just general Sioux Falls experience — ask for examples from the last six to twelve months.
- **Communication style and availability**, since responsiveness matters significantly during time-sensitive offer situations.

## Questions worth asking

1. **"How many homes have you sold or helped buy in this specific neighborhood recently?"** Specific, recent local experience beats general metro-wide experience.
2. **"What's your marketing plan if I'm selling?"** Photography quality and listing promotion genuinely affect time on market and final price.
3. **"How do you communicate during the process — text, call, email — and how quickly should I expect a response?"** Mismatched expectations here cause real frustration later.
4. **"Can you walk me through your commission structure and what's included?"**

## Red flags

- Reluctance to discuss or verify licensing
- No specific recent examples of local transactions
- Vague or evasive answers about commission structure
- Pressure to sign an exclusive agreement before you've had a real conversation about approach

## Why interviewing more than one agent is normal

For sellers especially, talking to two or three agents before choosing is standard practice, not an insult to anyone involved. It's the most reliable way to compare marketing approach, local knowledge, and communication style before making a decision that affects one of the largest transactions most people make.
