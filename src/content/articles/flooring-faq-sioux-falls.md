## What flooring is best for a Sioux Falls basement?

Vinyl plank or tile are generally recommended over solid hardwood, given seasonal groundwater and moisture pattern shifts in below-grade spaces here.

## Why do I see small gaps in my hardwood floor every winter?

Dry indoor air from heating season causes solid hardwood to contract slightly, creating normal seasonal gaps that typically close back up as summer humidity returns.

## How much does laminate flooring cost installed?

Typically $4-$8 per square foot including standard installation, though subfloor condition and old-flooring removal can add to the total.

## Should I run a humidifier to protect my floors?

Many homeowners with solid hardwood do, targeting 30-50% relative humidity during heating season to reduce seasonal board gapping.

## Does removing old flooring cost extra?

Usually yes — tear-out and disposal is typically a separate line item from new installation, and can be significant with tile removal specifically.

## Is engineered hardwood better than solid hardwood for Sioux Falls homes?

For most spaces, yes, due to better dimensional stability against our seasonal humidity swings — solid hardwood still works well in climate-controlled main living areas, but engineered is often the safer choice overall.
