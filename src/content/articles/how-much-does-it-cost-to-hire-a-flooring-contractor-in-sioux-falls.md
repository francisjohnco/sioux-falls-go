Flooring costs in Sioux Falls vary widely by material, but as a starting point: laminate typically runs $4-$8 per square foot installed, engineered hardwood $6-$12, solid hardwood $8-$15+, and luxury vinyl plank $5-$9 — all including standard installation labor.

## Typical price ranges by material

- **Laminate:** $4-$8/sq ft installed
- **Luxury vinyl plank (LVP):** $5-$9/sq ft installed
- **Engineered hardwood:** $6-$12/sq ft installed
- **Solid hardwood:** $8-$15+/sq ft installed
- **Tile:** $7-$14/sq ft installed, more for intricate patterns

## Why humidity swings matter for material choice here

Sioux Falls has real seasonal humidity swings — dry winters with indoor heating running constantly, more humid summers. Solid hardwood expands and contracts with those swings more than engineered hardwood or vinyl, which is part of why many local flooring pros steer homeowners toward engineered options for long-term stability, particularly in additions or rooms without great climate control.

## What actually moves the price

- **Subfloor condition.** An uneven or damaged subfloor needs leveling or repair before new flooring goes down — a cost that's easy to miss in a rough estimate.
- **Removal and disposal of old flooring**, especially tile, which is more labor-intensive to remove than carpet or laminate.
- **Below-grade installation.** Basement flooring often requires a moisture barrier, adding to material and labor cost.

## Getting a fair quote

Ask whether the quote includes subfloor prep and old-flooring removal, or whether those are separate line items that could be added later. A flooring quote that seems unusually low compared to others is often missing one of these steps.
