Whittier is built around its namesake park, a large open space with playgrounds, picnic shelters, and seasonal soccer leagues that keep the neighborhood genuinely active. It sits close enough to downtown for an easy commute, while still feeling like its own distinct community.

The neighborhood provides quick access to the Big Sioux River Greenway and downtown employers, making it a practical, well-located choice for people who want proximity without downtown's density.
