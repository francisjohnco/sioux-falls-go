Downtown Sioux Falls centers on Phillips Avenue, a stretch of restored 1800s brick buildings now filled with restaurants, boutiques, and the rotating outdoor art of SculptureWalk. It's the city's most walkable district by far, anchored at one end by Falls Park and threaded throughout with genuine historic character.

The neighborhood is also home to the Denny Sanford PREMIER Center, the Levitt at the Falls summer concert series, and a growing residential population living in converted lofts and new apartments right above the storefronts.
