The McKennan Park neighborhood grew up around its namesake park, home to century-old trees, manicured gardens, a historic bandshell, and a summer swimming pool. The surrounding streets are lined with well-kept historic homes, and the area is consistently described by residents as a peaceful, established part of the city.

It sits near Terrace Park's Shoto-Teien Japanese Gardens and is served by highly-rated Lincoln High School, making it a popular choice for families who want history and green space close to home.
